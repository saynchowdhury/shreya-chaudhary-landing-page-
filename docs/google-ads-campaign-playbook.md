Operational blueprint to launch, manage, and scale high-converting Google Search campaigns for **Shreya Chaudhary Makeup**.

## 7. Dynamic WhatsApp Conversion Tracking & URL Parameters

Tag all ads with dynamic Google Ads value-track parameters to track exact keyword performance and location of physical intent:

```text
{lpurl}?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=winter2026_bridal&utm_adgroup={adgroupid}&utm_content={loc_physical_ms}&utm_term={keyword}
```

### Weekly Search Terms Management:
1. Every Tuesday, open Google Ads > **Insights & Reports** > **Search Terms**.
2. Filter by `Clicks >= 1` and sort by `Conversions`.
3. Add any high-converting queries as **Exact Match** `[...]` into their respective regional ad group.
4. Add any query containing terms like `parlour`, `academy`, `cheap`, or `lehenga` to the **Universal Negative List**.
